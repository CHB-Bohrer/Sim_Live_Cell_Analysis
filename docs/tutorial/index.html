Sim Live Cell Analysis · a narrated tour

A guided, animated walk through the whole program and its dashboard: what each stage of the pipeline does, how to start the dashboard, how to make a simulation, and what every page shows and how to use it. At the end of each part there is a short Your turn list to try in the real dashboard. About 30 minutes in total; every part can be jumped to from the list on the left.

The voice comes from your browser. Microsoft Edge offers the most natural voices (look for names ending in “Online (Natural)”, e.g. Aria or Jenny; they need an internet connection). Chrome has Google voices, Safari its own. If no voice is listed, the captions still run at reading speed.

Keys: Space = play/pause · ← / → = previous/next sentence · Shift + ← / → = previous/next scene · M = voice on/off · C = captions
